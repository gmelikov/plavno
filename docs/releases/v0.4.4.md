## Efficiency

- **Audio cache indicator.** The footer shows the bytes cached for the current,
  previous, and next tracks, using compact units such as `760K` and `1.01M`.
  Updates reuse the terminal scene and keep the volume and speed controls in
  place. In an eight-second Linux PTY test with a slow stream and a 200×60
  terminal, scene rebuilds dropped from 35 to 5 and median player CPU time from
  180 ms to 110 ms.
- **Sixel buffers.** The renderer reuses its surface, trails, and encoding
  buffers between frames. The HiDPI test allocated the dancer surface once
  instead of 186 times. Median player CPU time stayed at 540 ms over six
  seconds in that test.
- **Playback history.** New plays append one line to `history.jsonl` without a
  per-play `fsync`. The player periodically compacts the log and keeps up to
  2,000 recent plays. It skips truncated records and removes `history.json`
  after a successful migration.

Large covers are reduced to a thumbnail during decoding. These Linux fixture
measurements used 2048×2048 covers:

| Format | Memory metric | Before → after | Median decoding time |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| PNG | Largest allocation | 16 MiB → 432 KiB | 43 → 41 ms |
| JPEG | Peak heap | 13.0 MB → 1.0 MB | 43 → 23 ms |
| Lossy WebP | Peak heap | 19.5 MB → 0.43 MB | 202 → 27 ms |
| Lossless WebP | Peak heap | About 17.2 MB in both runs | 106 → 41 ms |

## Dance

The dancer can use two embedded motion-capture phrases. Heel and toe contacts
improve foot placement, and the dance reacts to the audio at any playback
volume.
